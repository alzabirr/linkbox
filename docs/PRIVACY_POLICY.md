# Linkly – Privacy policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Linkly ("the app") is made by [YOUR NAME]. This policy explains what the app
does with your information. Short version: **nothing leaves your phone.**

## What the app stores
- The links you save, their titles, summaries and pictures.
- Your collections and settings.

All of this is stored **only on your phone**, in the app's private storage.
There is no account, no sign-in and no cloud server.

## What the app sends over the internet
When you save a link, the app opens that web page to read its title, summary
and picture (the "preview"), the same way a browser would. The website you
saved can see that a phone visited it. The app sends nothing else, and never
sends your other links or any personal information.

## What the app does not do
- No ads.
- No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
- No selling or sharing of data. The developer never receives your data.

## Clipboard
When you open the app, it checks once whether you copied a link, so it can
offer to save it. Android shows a small notice when this happens. The copied
text is not stored unless you tap **Save link**.

## Backups and exports
**Back up now** and **Export** write files to the `Download/Linkly` folder
on your phone. Only you can move or share those files.

## Deleting your data
Delete links or collections in the app, or uninstall the app to remove
everything it stored. Backup files in `Download/Linkly` stay until you
delete them.

## Children
The app is not aimed at children and collects no data from anyone.
